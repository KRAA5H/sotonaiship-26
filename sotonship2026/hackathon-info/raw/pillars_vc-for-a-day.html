▚Pillar 04

VC for a Day

Back the builders

Spend it wisely, guv'nor.

Everyone in the room becomes an investor for the afternoon. Walk the demo fair, kick the tyres, and back the teams you believe in with virtual capital.

1

Get funded

Every attendee is issued virtual capital when submissions close on Sunday.

2

Due diligence

Walk the demo fair. Watch the demos, ask hard questions, talk to the builders.

3

Back your picks

Allocate your capital to the projects you'd invest in. Anyone's team but your own.

4

People's Choice

The team that raises the most wins the People's Choice prize, announced at the awards.

* Virtual capital — not real money. It decides the People's Choice prize and is separate from the judges' scores.
