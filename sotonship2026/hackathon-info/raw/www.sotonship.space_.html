SotonShip26

Stop learning AI. Start shipping it. · EAGLE LABS · 16–18 OCT 2026

A free 48-hour overnight applied-AI hackathon for 120 student builders, with three partner-set tracks, Saturday workshops and talks, mentors on the floor, and a live demo fair on Sunday. The venue stays open through both nights, so you can hack and sleep on site.

We're no longer accepting applications for Soton Ship 2026.
