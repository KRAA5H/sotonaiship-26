# hackathon-info

Everything the organisers published, pulled **16 October 2026, 18:30 to 18:45 BST** from:

- `live.sotonship.space` — the live event portal (schedule, tracks, judging, help, Q&A, login)
- `www.sotonship.space` — the pre-event site (mission, pillars, manifest, backers, FAQ, conduct, privacy)
- `live.sotonship.space/briefs/*.pdf` — the three track briefs as PDFs

Raw HTML and extracted text of every page sit in `raw/`. The PDFs and their text sit in `briefs/`.

| File | What it holds |

| `rules-and-fair-play.md` | Event-wide rules, track rules, conduct code, reporting routes, privacy |
| `judging-and-scoring.md` | The Ship test, the five weighted criteria, the scoring scale, VC for a Day maths, the Stage 2 panel, track-specific lenses |

| `tracks-overview.md` | All three tracks side by side, with partners and what each rewards |
| `track-southampton-city.md` | Full Southampton City brief |
| `track-fintech.md` | Full FinTech brief |
| `track-responsible-ai.md` | Full Responsible AI brief, with the papers it cites |

| `project-directions.md` | The exact starting points the organisers say they want to see, per track |
| `venue-and-contacts.md` | Getting there, what to bring, who to ask, emergencies |
| `source-index.md` | Every URL, when it was fetched, what it held, and what has changed |
| `raw/` | Raw HTML plus text extracts, per page. The audit trail for anything above. |
| `briefs/` | The three track brief PDFs plus `pdftotext` extracts |

## Freshness, stated plainly

- The **live portal is the current truth**: schedule with real times, the three tracks with full briefs, submission requirements, the judging page.
- The **`www` site is partly stale** (last updated 12 September 2026, before the final tracks). Where the two disagree, `source-index.md` says which is newer. The headline stale item: `www` describes Responsible AI as "an AI Cyber Arena with Holistic AI, upload a defence and an attack", while the live brief is a product-and-business brief. Treat the live brief as current.
- The live schedule page carries the banner "every event on this board is subject to change". The printed manifest handed out at the desk is the one that counts.
