## The organisers' stated reason for the event

From `www.sotonship.space/mission`: UK universities teach AI theory, not AI entrepreneurship, and the industry wants builders. The weekend is 48 overnight hours across three applied tracks set with industry and public-sector partners, plus workshops, talks, mentors and a demo fair.

## Who is in the room

- **Users and buyers to design for** (from the briefs): residents, council officers, local businesses, port and logistics workers, cruise passengers, new students; and in finance, bank and fintech staff in fraud, payments, compliance and KYC, small businesses and sole traders, remittance and wallet users, wallet agents and kiosk operators.
- **Judges and mentors** come from the partner organisations, which include a bank, a payments provider, a council and an AI assurance company. The prize terms say non-cash prizes may include follow-on support and conversations with partners about internships.

## What participants keep

Participants keep full ownership of everything built at SotonShip. The organisers, the AI Society, the University, Hackathons UK and the partners claim no IP. Open-sourcing is encouraged, never required. Partners get a demo and nothing more.
