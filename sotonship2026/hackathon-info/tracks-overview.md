# The three tracks

Source: `live.sotonship.space/tracks` and the three track pages. Fetched 16 October 2026.

Each team enters **one track only**. There is no fixed list of projects in any track: you choose the problem and must show it matters.

| | Southampton City | FinTech | Responsible AI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full brief | `track-southampton-city.md` | `track-fintech.md` | `track-responsible-ai.md` |
| One line | An AI product for a real person in Southampton | An AI product for someone who saves, spends, sends or manages money | A product that makes AI useful and trustworthy for a specific person, pitched as a business |
| Set with | Southampton City Council | JPMorganChase, Deutsche Bank, TrueMoney | RAi UK research; historically with Holistic AI |
| Data | No compulsory dataset. Public Southampton data: flooding, tides, buses, air quality, traffic, cruise schedules, council services | No compulsory dataset. Public data on companies, sanctions, remittance costs, exchange rates, and labelled fraud and money-laundering transactions | No compulsory dataset, model or framework |
| Signals it rewards | Local evidence, real local data with source and date, a plausible owner-and-funder answer, a path to a second city | A costed error trade-off, baseline comparison, how it plugs into existing systems, whether a partner bank could adopt it | A specific need with evidence, a tested AI capability or assurance function, reproducible tests, a measured benefit and its trade-off |
| Working product weight | 25% in every track | 25% | 25% |
| What does not fit | A general Southampton chatbot; a map or prediction that does not lead to a decision; an existing product with AI bolted on; anything needing restricted council systems, real personal data, port access or hardware without prior organiser agreement | A general finance chatbot; a trading bot or price prediction that does not lead to a decision; an existing product with AI bolted on; anything needing real customer data, live bank credentials or real money movement without prior agreement | A generic chatbot, ethics checklist or dashboard with no specific user, decision or action; unsupported claims of being fair, safe or compliant; an earlier project resubmitted; anything needing restricted systems or real sensitive personal data |

## How the three differ in practice

- **Southampton City** is judged partly on place: the extra questions are about impact on Southampton and on named groups in the city, whether the data is genuinely Southampton data with dates and sources, who in the local system would own and fund it, whether it works for people who are less digital or have access needs, and whether it avoids sending non-public council information to external AI services.
- **FinTech** is judged partly on the cost of being wrong: how often it is wrong and what each error costs (false alarms vs missed cases), whether it was tested against a simple baseline, whether a partner bank could realistically adopt it, and whether it can explain a decision to a customer or a regulator under rules such as FCA Consumer Duty, anti-money-laundering and data protection.
- **Responsible AI** treats responsibility as evidence across all five criteria rather than one section: a measured improvement and its trade-off, one demonstrated failure and what happens next, who can review or override the output, and what evidence is still missing before real deployment.

## The one-sentence tests the briefs set

- Southampton City: *We help [a specific person] to [do or decide something] in [part of Southampton] using [AI and the data behind it].*
- FinTech: *We help [a specific person] to [do or decide something] in [part of finance] using [AI and the data behind it].*
- Responsible AI: *We help [a specific person] to [do or decide something] using [AI or an AI assurance tool], while reducing [a specific risk to people].*

## What every project in every track must show

- **A real problem**, with a named user, how often it happens, and what they do today, backed by evidence such as data, a regulation or a conversation.
- **AI the product depends on.** If removing the AI leaves the product doing its main job, AI is not at the core and the idea needs rethinking.
- **A product that works on real data**, end to end and live, with sources and dates visible. Synthetic data is fine for testing if labelled.
- **A business case**: who pays, roughly what it costs per user including AI costs, and what it would take to reach a second customer or market.
- **Honesty about limits**, including at least one failure case shown in the demo.
