# Schedule

Source: `live.sotonship.space/schedule`, fetched 16 October 2026. All times UK time (BST). The page carries the banner "every event on this board is subject to change" and the printed manifest handed out at the desk is the one that counts.

**Hacking runs non-stop from Friday 20:00 to Sunday 13:00, including Friday and Saturday nights, at Eagle Labs.**

## The two hard deadlines

| Deadline | What |
|---|---|
| **Sunday 18 October, 13:00** | Hacking stops and submissions lock. The website locks all submissions at 13:00 sharp. A team whose submission is incomplete at the lock is not shown in Stage 1 |
| **Sunday 18 October, 12:00** | "Final countdown" hour starts: finish the video, the summary and the README |

Everything else on Sunday after 13:40 is pitching, judging and awards, not build time.

## Getting the time-sensitive bits right

- If a demo needs to be recorded, it has to be recordable by 12:00 on Sunday, and YouTube upload plus a link on the team page before 13:00. There is no extension except "for a team in special circumstances", at the organisers' discretion.
- Stage 1 pitches run in random order per track on Sunday afternoon and need a 1:00 to 3:00 video plus up to 3:00 of pitch, then 1:00 of Q&A.
