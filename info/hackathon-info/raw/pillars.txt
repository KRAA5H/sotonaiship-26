# The Pillars
◀ Back to deck
▚The pillars
## Select a level.
Four strands, one weekend. Enter a pillar to see how it plays.
01Level 01
### The Tracks
One main hack across three partner-set tracks. Pick a real problem, build a working solution, demo it live to judges.
▶ Press start
02Level 02
### Workshops
Guided, hands-on sessions on Saturday, from beginner-friendly to advanced. Subjects are still being set.
▶ Press start
03Level 03
### Talks
Short, high-signal talks from industry and research. 20 minutes + 5 min Q&A, no slide padding.
▶ Press start
04Level 04
### VC for a Day
Everyone in the room becomes an investor for the afternoon and backs the teams they believe in with virtual capital. The People's Choice prize follows the money.
▶ Press start
