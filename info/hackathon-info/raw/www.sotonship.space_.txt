# SotonShip26
Stop learning AI. Start shipping it. · EAGLE LABS · 16–18 OCT 2026
A free 48-hour overnight applied-AI hackathon for 120 student builders, with three partner-set tracks, Saturday workshops and talks, mentors on the floor, and a live demo fair on Sunday. The venue stays open through both nights, so you can hack and sleep on site.
We're no longer accepting applications for Soton Ship 2026.
Log inView the manifest
Already confirmed? Log in to access the live event portal. This is a 48-hour overnight event: the venue stays open from Friday 17:00 to Sunday evening and you can sleep on site, so there is no need to book accommodation. Read our Code of Conduct.

## Four strands. One weekend.
Level select ▶
01[01/04]
### The Tracks
One main hack across three partner-set tracks. Pick a real problem, build a working solution, demo it live to judges.
▶ Enter
02[02/04]
### Workshops
Guided, hands-on sessions on Saturday, from beginner-friendly to advanced. Subjects are still being set.
▶ Enter
03[03/04]
### Talks
Short, high-signal talks from industry and research. 20 minutes + 5 min Q&A, no slide padding.
▶ Enter
04[04/04]
### VC for a Day
Everyone in the room becomes an investor for the afternoon and backs the teams they believe in with virtual capital. The People's Choice prize follows the money.
