## The opportunity
The Problem
UK universities teach AI theory, not AI entrepreneurship. Students graduate knowing AI but not building it. The industry is desperate for builders.
Our Solution
A free 48-hour overnight hack across three applied tracks set with industry and public-sector partners, plus workshops, talks, mentors on the floor, and a demo fair where you show what you shipped.
The Crew
120 student builders from UK universities — a deliberate mix of courses, universities and experience levels, with places held for first-time hackers.
Who it's for / What you'll leave with
Ambitious students who want to build, not just study. You leave with a shipped portfolio project, a denser network, and direct exposure to the partners judging and mentoring on the floor.
