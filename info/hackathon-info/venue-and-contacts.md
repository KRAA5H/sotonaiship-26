# Venue, logistics and contacts

Sources: `www.sotonship.space/manifest`, `www.sotonship.space/faq`, `live.sotonship.space/help`. Fetched 16 October 2026.

## The venue

**Barclays Eagle Labs (Network Eagle Lab)**, Marlands Shopping Centre, second floor, Portland Terrace, Southampton SO14 7SJ.

- Street-level entrance on Portland Terrace, **opposite Quilter**.
- Second floor, **lift access**, one accessible toilet. Everything happens on one floor.
- Emails for access needs: aisoc@soton.ac.uk, sorted before arrival.
- **No event parking and no disabled parking on site.** Nearby paid car parks: Marlands, West Quay and NCP. Arrive on foot or by public transport.
- Bike storage and showers are on site.
- The venue is a working space, so it has to be left as found.

## Getting there

| Mode | Detail |
|---|---|
| Train | Southampton Central is 10–15 minutes on foot. Direct services from London Waterloo, Bristol, Portsmouth and Bournemouth |
| Bus | Routes 1, 2, 506, U1C, U6C, W1 and X11 stop nearby. U1C and U6C are the Unilink buses from the university |

## Overnight

- **It is an overnight event.** Eagle Labs stays open from doors at 17:00 Friday until 20:30 Sunday, and sleeping on site is allowed. No accommodation needs booking.
- Going home or to a hotel for the night is fine; tell the registration desk when leaving and coming back.
- The live schedule confirms hacking runs non-stop through Friday and Saturday nights.
- The quiet room is open all weekend, no questions asked, for a break, a call home, prayer or silence.

## What to bring

- Laptop and charger.
- **Photo student ID** for the desk. The registration desk only admits people on the confirmed list.
- Comfort items for an overnight: sleeping bag or blanket, toothbrush, hoodie, water bottle, power strip.

## Logging in to the live portal

`live.sotonship.space` — two routes:

1. **Email code:** enter the email used to register on Luma, and a 6-digit code is emailed.
2. **Badge code:** the access code printed on the badge (format like `ABCD-EFGH`, not case-sensitive).

Trouble logging in: ask at the help desk or email aisoc@soton.ac.uk. Submissions, the team page, the Q&A board and the VC for a Day investing all run behind this login.
