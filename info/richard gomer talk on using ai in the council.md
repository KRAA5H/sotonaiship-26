	- civic ai values
		- effective
			- works and delivers real improvement - not just change things
		- inclusive
			- benefits are shared across society and communities
		- transparent
			- we can see how AI is used and how it works
		- accountable
			- someone can answer how AI is used and its impact
			- elected representatives are still the ones setting directoin
		- connecting
			- should be enhancing the connection and collaboration between people and communities
	- dangers
		- overselling AI
			- not magic - hard problems are still hard
			- incremental improvements in traffic light timing wont solve congestion
		- ignoring the public
			- AI is controversial and congested
			- real risk of ignoring those people
			- bring everyone with us
		- underestimating councils
			- councils are sophisticated organisations with access to specialise expertise and multi-million pound budgets
			- ai needs to provide a clear benefit over what exists currently
	- pitching civic ai
		- pitch against the civic ai values
		- effective
			- what does this system do
			- who will use it
			- what evidence will demonstrate that it is better than the status quo
		- inclusive
			- how does the system benefit people>
			- how are the benefits shared?
			- how do we avoid creating new exclusions
		- connecting
			- which relationships will this system change?
			- which people will still want or need to communicate and how will they do so?
		- transparent
			- how will people be informed about this system?
			- how will people understand the impact of this system?
		- accountable
			- who will make the decision to use the system>
			- how will they monitor and shape the ways that it is used?
		- acceptable
			- what will different groups of people think about this system?
			- what reassurance will they want?
